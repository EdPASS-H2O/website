Web/PDF Resources

Many resources about the water industry can be found online. Click the link below to access a compiled list of web and PDF resources.

Below you will find a compiled list of online resources. Clicking each button will open a new window with the corresponding webpage as detailed in the description beneath it.

USGS Water Science School

The U.S. Geological Survey's Water Science School provides much information about water, as well as pictures, maps, data, and interactive tests and activities. 

Texas A&M AgriLife Extension (OWTS)

The Texas A&M AgriLife Extension Onight Wastewater Treatment Systems page provides information about their wastewater treatment processes, including helpful graphics and detailed information about each component in the process. 

CDC Drinking Water Regulations

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention website provides information about their drinking water standards and regulations, including detailed information about the Safe Drinking Water Act. 

Work for Water

Work for Water's website provides resources to those looking to find a job or start a career in the water industry. The site was developed by the American Water Works Association and the Water Environment Federation. 

Occupational Outlook Handbook

This webpage on the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics website includes general career information about Water and Wastewater System Operators such as median pay, average education level, on-the-job training, etc. 

EPA Water Topics

The Water Topics Page on the Environmental Protection Agency's website provides information about their efforts to keep sources of drinking water clean. 

NGWA Groundwater Fundamentals

The National Groundwater Association's Website provides information about the fundamentals of groundwater, as well as general information about their work. 

Oklahoma Drinking Water Compliance Monitoring

The Oklahoma Environmental Quality's Drinking Water Compliance Monitoring webpage provides information about rules that keep drinking water clean and resources for evaluating one's drinking water sample.

AWWA Source Water Protection

Advance CTE Career Cluster

This webpage provides information about pursuing a career in agriculture, food, and natural resources. It includes PDF resources about Common Core standards, plans of study, etc.

UM Explore Resources

This webpage by the University of Manitoba offers information about a wide range of occupations, as well as career planning support, employment resources, and general career services.
